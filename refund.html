Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy applies to registrations for the Mystic Trinity – Osho Meditation Retreat, held 24–27 December 2026 at the School of Ancient Wisdom, Devanahalli, Bengaluru. Registrations are managed by Sw. Toshen.

Cancellation by a participant

To cancel, call or WhatsApp +91 99131 56907 with your name and registration details. The date we receive your request decides the refund:

Cancellation receivedRefund
On or before 9 December 2026
(15 or more days before the retreat)		Full fee, less an administration charge of ₹ 1,000
10 – 17 December 2026
(7 to 14 days before the retreat)		50% of the fee paid
From 18 December 2026, or no-showNo refund

No refund is given for partial attendance or for leaving the retreat early.

Transfer of registration

If you cannot attend, you may transfer your registration to another person at no charge. Please tell us the new participant's name and phone number before the retreat begins.

Cancellation or changes by the organiser

If the retreat is cancelled by the organiser, every participant receives a full refund of the fee paid. If the dates or venue change, you may either accept the new arrangement or cancel for a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method, or by bank transfer to the account you paid from, within 7–10 working days of approval.
