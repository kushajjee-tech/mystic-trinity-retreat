About the camp

Many traditions explore breath and meditation. Osho brings together profound insights from Yoga, Tantra and Tao: three distinct doorways into awareness and self-realisation.

In this unique workshop, Sw. Chaitanya Keerti shares Osho's insights through breathwork and meditation.

What to expect

  • Guided breathwork and meditation
  • Insights from Yoga, Tantra and Tao
  • Deeper awareness and self-exploration
  • A space for stillness and inner discovery
