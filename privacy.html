Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Sw. Toshen, organiser of the Mystic Trinity – Osho Meditation Retreat, collects and uses personal information.

Information we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or tracking. We collect personal information only when you contact us to register, which may include:

  • your name, phone number and city;
  • your accommodation choice and payment confirmation details;
  • any health information you choose to share so we can support you during the retreat.

We do not collect or store card numbers or bank login details. Payments are processed by your bank or payment provider under their own privacy terms.

How we use it

We use your information only to manage your registration, communicate with you about the retreat, arrange accommodation, process refunds, and keep you safe during the retreat. We do not sell or rent your information.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the retreat: with the venue (School of Ancient Wisdom) for accommodation, with the facilitator where relevant to your participation, with payment providers to process payments and refunds, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep registration records for as long as needed to run the retreat and meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete them.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may record technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. We do not use cookies or analytics.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the number below.
